# CargoFlow - Known Limitations & Assumptions

## Current Assumptions & Scope
1. **Network Focus**: Current seed data covers MSRTC Nashik Division (10 Depots, 50 Stops, 25 Routes, 30 Buses, 60 Trips).
2. **GPS Tracking**: Simulated real-time bus movement along geofenced route polylines for demo purposes.
3. **Weight Limits**: Bus cargo holds range from 40kg (Ordinary buses) to 80kg (Shivshahi/E-Shivai buses).
