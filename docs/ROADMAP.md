# CargoFlow - Product Roadmap

## Future Enhancements
- **Multi-Division Expansion**: Scaling from Nashik division to all 31 MSRTC divisions across Maharashtra.
- **Dynamic Pricing Engine**: Demand-based surge pricing for high-demand holiday routes.
- **Cold Chain Sensor Integration**: IoT temperature and humidity telemetry for agricultural cargo (grapes, onions, flowers).
- **Automated Payouts**: Automated escrow release to MSRTC depots upon verified delivery scan.
